Know yourself

Don’t know your dosha yet?

Ayurveda describes three energies — vata, pitta and kapha — that shape body and mind. Your own balance of them is your prakriti.

Air + Space

Vata

Creative, quick and light — but prone to dryness, restlessness and irregular digestion when out of balance.

  • Movement
  • Creativity
  • Circulation
Fire + Water

Pitta

Sharp, driven and warm — but prone to acidity, irritability and skin flare-ups when aggravated.

  • Digestion
  • Metabolism
  • Focus
Earth + Water

Kapha

Steady, calm and strong — but prone to sluggishness, congestion and weight gain when it grows heavy.

  • Stability
  • Immunity

Dinacharya

Four daily rituals from the classical texts

Small habits, practised daily, are the foundation of Ayurvedic health — long before any formulation.

  1. 01On waking

    Ushapan — warm water

    A glass of warm water before anything else gently wakes digestion and supports elimination.

  2. 02Before brushing

    Gandusha — oil pulling

    Swish a spoonful of sesame or coconut oil for a few minutes to cleanse the mouth, as the Charaka Samhita describes.

  3. 03Before bathing

    Abhyanga — self-massage

    Warm oil massaged into the skin calms vata, nourishes the body and grounds the mind.

  4. 04At night

    Nidra — early to bed

    Sleep before 10 pm, in kapha time, when rest comes easiest. A cup of golden milk helps you wind down.
